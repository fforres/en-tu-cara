title: Set your own reminders and snooze
---

En Tu Cara 0.10.1 — reminders and snooze are now yours to set (macOS, Apple Silicon).

Until now every meeting got one alert 5 minutes ahead, and snooze was a fixed set of buttons. This release makes both configurable, in Settings → Alerts.

What's new:
- **Your own reminder schedule** — keep the one 5-minute heads-up, change its timing, or add up to two more (say 30 and 10 minutes before). Don't want any early warning? Remove them all.
- **A separate snooze duration** — "Remind me again" now uses a single time you pick, independent from your reminders. Set reminders at 30 and 5 minutes before but snooze for 20 — they no longer have to match.

One thing that never changes: you still always get an alert the moment a meeting starts, even if you've turned off every early reminder. That one can't be disabled — it's the whole point.
