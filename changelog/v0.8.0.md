En Tu Cara 0.8.0 — alerts that survive losing calendar access (macOS, Apple Silicon).

This release came out of a real incident: macOS can corrupt an app's calendar permission so that access LOOKS granted but every read returns nothing — and re-granting doesn't fix it. 0.8.0 makes sure a meeting still alerts even while that's happening:

Alerts keep firing through an outage:
- If calendar reads start failing mid-session, En Tu Cara now keeps alerting from the last events it successfully read (up to 48h worth) instead of going quiet. The tray shows those events with a clear "may be stale" note, so you know what you're looking at.
- Meetings created AFTER access broke can't be seen until access heals — everything seen at least once keeps alerting on time.

The app repairs its own permission:
- When access is granted but reads persistently fail (the corrupted-permission case), En Tu Cara resets its own calendar permission record and asks you to grant access fresh — one system prompt, at most once every 6 hours. Settings also gets a manual "Repair access" button.
- The banners now tell you which situation you're in: "repairing automatically, events may be stale" vs "access was revoked — grant it again."

No more freezes when macOS misbehaves:
- A wedged macOS calendar service used to be able to freeze the whole app (spinning beach ball, empty alert overlay). Calendar reads are now time-boxed, so a stuck system service can't take the alerts down with it.
