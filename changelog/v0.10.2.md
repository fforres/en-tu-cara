title: Alerts survive sleep and display changes
---

En Tu Cara 0.10.2 — an alert can no longer outlive the window showing it (macOS, Apple Silicon).

If your Mac went to sleep while an alert was on screen, you could wake up to the worst possible version of this app: the alarm sound still going, no alert anywhere to be found, and no way to stop it except force-quitting En Tu Cara. Reported, reproduced, fixed.

What's fixed:
- **Sleeping through an alert no longer strands it.** An alert you haven't dismissed is now re-checked continuously, and if its window was lost while the Mac slept, it comes straight back — with its Join, Snooze and Dismiss buttons — instead of leaving a sound you can't silence.
- **The alert follows your screens.** Close the lid, unplug a monitor, or switch resolution while an alert is up, and it re-places itself onto a display you can actually see. Previously it could be left sitting at coordinates that no longer belonged to any screen — audible, but invisible.
- **Esc and Enter keep working after a display change.** Keyboard focus now moves to whichever copy of the alert is on your main screen, so dismissing without reaching for the mouse doesn't quietly stop working when your setup changes.
- **Dismissing always sticks.** A dismissal that landed at the same instant as one of those re-checks could previously bring the alert back from the dead, blank and still beeping. It can't anymore.

One known edge, so it doesn't surprise you: if you plug in a *new* display while an alert is already on screen, that display won't get its own copy of the alert until the next one fires. Every screen you already had stays covered and usable throughout.
