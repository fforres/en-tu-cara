En Tu Cara 0.4.4 — now properly signed, plus calendar-sync and menu-bar fixes (macOS, Apple Silicon).

Signing & install:
- The app is now signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens with a normal double-click — no more "unidentified developer" warning or right-click-to-open.
- From this release on, your calendar permission survives app updates instead of resetting (the app now has a stable signing identity).

Calendar accuracy:
- Events you delete or edit elsewhere (Google, Calendar.app) now disappear/update in the tray and alerts right away — reads sync instead of serving a stale snapshot.
- The menu-bar "next event" text now updates at the same moment as the event list when you open the tray, instead of lagging behind.

Permissions:
- If calendar access ever needs re-granting, the app now prompts you automatically on launch instead of making you find the "Grant calendar access" button.

Reliability:
- Tray reads preserve what's on screen through a transient calendar hiccup, the full-screen alert's "Join" button retries instead of dropping, and ENTUCARA_LOG=debug captures detailed logs when something looks off.
