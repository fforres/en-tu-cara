title: One alert per meeting, even across accounts
---

En Tu Cara 0.9.0 — one alert per meeting, even across accounts (macOS, Apple Silicon).

If you've shared a calendar between accounts — a work calendar mirrored into a personal Gmail, or you're subscribed to a few teammates' calendars — the same meeting used to show up several times: duplicate rows in the menu, and worse, a separate full-screen alert for each copy. 0.9.0 treats those as one meeting.

One meeting, one alert:
- A meeting that lives on more than one calendar or account is now collapsed to a single entry everywhere — the menu list, the menu-bar next-event title, and the full-screen alert. No more double takeovers for the same meeting.

See where a meeting comes from:
- The full-screen alert now names which of your synced accounts a meeting is on. Copies that come from subscribed coworkers' calendars under the same account fold into that one account, so it stays readable — while a meeting genuinely shared across separate accounts lists each one.

Alerts with a lot going on stay usable:
- When several meetings fire at once, the full-screen alert now scrolls instead of cutting cards off the bottom of the screen.
