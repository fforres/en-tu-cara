En Tu Cara 0.7.0 — alerts never fail silently, plus better diagnostics (macOS, Apple Silicon).

Never miss an alert to a quiet failure:
- If En Tu Cara loses access to your calendar while running (a permission reset, a stale connection after sleep), it no longer just goes quiet. You get a notification, a ⚠️ marker in the menu bar, and a banner in Settings — so you know alerts are paused instead of finding out the hard way.
- It also tries to fix itself: it refreshes its calendar connection, re-checks after your Mac wakes from sleep, and re-requests access if the grant was reset — recovering on its own where it can, and clearing the warning the moment access is back.

Diagnostics & support:
- A new "Export logs" button (Settings → Advanced) saves the app's local logs to your Downloads folder and copies them to the clipboard, so you can attach them to a bug report. Logs stay on your Mac and never include event titles or calendar contents.
- Under the hood, logging was rebuilt so issues are easier to pin down — including recording the app's own signing identity at startup, which is what made a tricky "calendar access kept resetting" bug diagnosable.
