En Tu Cara 0.6.0 — send suggestions right from the app (macOS, Apple Silicon).

Feedback:
- A new Comments button (💬) in the menu-bar popover opens a suggestion box, so you can send ideas, bug reports, or anything else without leaving En Tu Cara.
- Adding your email is optional — only if you'd like a reply — and feedback sends even when usage stats are turned off, since it's something you chose to send.
- Suggestions are delivered in the background and never block your alerts.
