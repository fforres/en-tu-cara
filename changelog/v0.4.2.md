En Tu Cara 0.4.2 — unmissable, fully-local meeting alerts for the macOS menu bar (Apple Silicon).

Calendar access now works reliably:
- The "Grant calendar access" prompt appears correctly (the app's hardened runtime had been silently blocking it), and access applies immediately — the app relaunches itself once you Allow, so events show right away.
- If access was previously denied, the button now sends you to System Settings instead of doing nothing.

Reliability:
- Fixed a freeze/beachball that could occur when opening the menu-bar popover.
- The alarm engine now survives unexpected errors instead of silently stopping — it keeps firing your alerts no matter what.
- Snoozes and dismissed alerts are saved atomically, so a crash can't lose them and cause a missed or repeated alert.

Tray & alerts:
- Cleaner event rows: title, time, and the calendar it comes from (account + sub-calendar). "Open videocall" opens the meeting link; clicking the row opens the event.
- Right-click an event for: Open in browser, Copy link, Open in local calendar, and Ignore this event (silences just that one occurrence — recurring siblings still alert).
- The full-screen alert now appears on EVERY display, not just the primary.
- Zero-length (0-minute) calendar entries now get a "starting now" alert.

Ad-hoc signed for now: on first launch right-click → Open (or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway) until notarization is set up.
