En Tu Cara 0.5.0 — a live menu-bar countdown, tray fixes, and optional anonymized usage stats (macOS, Apple Silicon).

Menu bar:
- The "next event" countdown in the menu bar now stays current on its own, ticking down every ~10 seconds against the live clock instead of only updating on the alarm poll.
- A finished event clears from the menu bar within ~10 seconds instead of lingering — fixes the frozen title that could leave a past meeting stuck up top.

Tray accuracy:
- The tray event list now respects your disabled calendars: events from a calendar you've turned off drop out of the list, matching what the alerts use. The tray, the menu-bar title, and the alarm scheduler now all read events through one shared path, so they can't disagree about what's coming up.

Usage stats (opt-out, anonymized):
- This release adds optional, anonymized telemetry to help spot crashes and missed-alert bugs — no event titles, attendees, or calendar contents are ever sent. It's on by default and you can turn it off anytime in Settings (or set ENTUCARA_TELEMETRY=off). Your identity is a random device id plus a one-way hash of your account, never your email.
