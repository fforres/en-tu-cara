En Tu Cara 0.10.0 — see what's changed, right in the app (macOS, Apple Silicon).

Until now an update just swapped itself in quietly. 0.10.0 makes every version's changes visible from inside En Tu Cara.

What's new, before you update:
- When an update is available, the About tab now shows the incoming version's release notes — what's actually changing — before you relaunch into it, instead of just a version number.

The full release history:
- Settings → About has a new "What's new" section listing every version En Tu Cara has shipped, newest first. Click any version to read its notes — handy for catching up on what changed while you weren't looking.
